### Project Description
Dialer is a revolutionary Android application designed to test the limits of human patience and social anxiety. It transforms the mundane task of making a phone call into a high-stakes psychological thriller, featuring deceptive UI patterns, reversed logic, and a "Random Dialer" algorithm that ensures you never actually talk to the person you intended to.
### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Standard phone apps are "too reliable" and "efficient," leading to a predictable life where you actually speak to the people you meant to call. This lack of surprise has resulted in a global epidemic of "Comfortable Calling," where users are far too confident in their ability to press a green button and expect a specific outcome.
### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We’ve engineered a multi-layered frustration engine to bring "The Spark of Chaos" back to your mobile device:
•

The Confirmation Gauntlet: A 4-step interrogation process that questions your eligibility and sanity before every call.
•

Deceptive UI: A grid of "Decline" buttons where only one answers, and a "Mute" button that strictly tells lies.
•

The Minefield: Three call buttons for every contact—only one is real, while the others deliver harsh existential truths via Toast notifications.
•

The Periodic Prankster: An automated system that rings your phone every 2 minutes with funny audio just to keep you on your toes.

Key "Features" Log:
•
Random Hijacker: Outgoing calls alternate between a real call to a random contact and a fake simulation that plays audio.
